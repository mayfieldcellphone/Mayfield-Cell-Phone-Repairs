Why Is Your MacBook Running Slow? Simple Fixes That Work

A slow MacBook is usually caused by low storage space, outdated software, or too many background apps running at once. In some cases, hardware issues like an aging battery or traditional hard drive can also reduce performance. The good news is that simple steps — such as freeing up storage, updating macOS, managing startup programs, can help.

Common Causes of Slow Performance

  • Low Storage Space: When your MacBook's storage is nearly full, it struggles to perform basic tasks.
  • Outdated Software: Old versions of macOS and apps can cause compatibility issues.
  • Too Many Background Processes: Apps running in the background consume resources.
  • Hardware Issues: Aging components like batteries or hard drives can slow things down.

Simple Fixes to Try

  1. Free Up Storage: Delete unnecessary files, empty the trash, and use built-in storage management tools.
  2. Update macOS: Go to System Settings > General > Software Update to install the latest version.
  3. Manage Startup Items: In System Settings > General > Login Items, remove unnecessary apps from startup.
  4. Restart Regularly: A simple restart can clear temporary files and refresh system performance.
  5. Check Activity Monitor: Use this tool to identify and quit resource-heavy processes.

If these steps don't help, it might be time to consider hardware upgrades or professional repair services.
