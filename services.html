Our Services

iPhone Repair Service

Comprehensive iPhone repair services for all models including iPhone 15, 14, 13, 12, SE, and older versions. Our services include screen replacements with genuine OLED displays, battery replacements for better performance, camera lens repairs, charging port fixes, Face ID repairs, and water damage restoration. We use only authentic Apple parts and provide warranty on all repairs.

iPad Repair Service

Expert iPad and tablet repair services for all models including iPad Pro (all sizes), iPad Air, iPad mini, and iPad. We handle LCD screen replacements, digitizer repairs, battery replacements, charging port fixes, home button repairs, and speaker/microphone replacements. Android tablets from Samsung, Google, and other brands are also welcome. All repairs include diagnostic testing and performance optimization.

MacBook Repair Service

Complete MacBook repair and maintenance services for MacBook Air, MacBook Pro (Intel and M1/M2/M3 chip), and classic MacBook. Our services include keyboard and trackpad replacements, Retina display repairs, SSD upgrades, RAM upgrades, battery replacements, logic board repairs, and liquid damage recovery. We also provide data recovery, virus removal, and performance optimization to keep your MacBook running smoothly.

Apple Watch Repair Service

Specialized Apple Watch repair services for all Series models (1-9, SE, Ultra). We provide screen replacements with genuine sapphire crystal displays, battery replacements for extended usage, band replacements and repairs, digital crown repairs, and water resistance restoration. Whether your watch has a cracked screen from a fall or needs a new battery, our technicians ensure your fitness and health tracking device is back to perfect condition.

Samsung Repair Service

Professional Samsung device repair services for Galaxy S, A, Note, Z Fold/Flip series, Galaxy Tab tablets, and Galaxy Watch wearables. Our expertise includes AMOLED screen replacements, battery replacements, motherboard repairs, camera module fixes, USB-C port repairs, and software troubleshooting. We work with all Samsung models and provide genuine Samsung parts where available to maintain device integrity and warranty compatibility.

Laptop Repair Service

Comprehensive laptop repair services for all major brands including Dell, HP, Lenovo, ASUS, Acer, Toshiba, and more. We handle LCD screen replacements, keyboard repairs, hard drive replacements and upgrades, RAM and SSD upgrades, motherboard repairs, cooling system cleaning, virus removal, data recovery, and operating system reinstallation. Whether it's a business laptop or personal device, we provide reliable repairs with fast turnaround times.
